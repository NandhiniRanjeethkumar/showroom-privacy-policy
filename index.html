Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Showroom Customer Assistant ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when customers communicate with our showroom through WhatsApp.

1. About the Service

Showroom Customer Assistant is a customer communication system designed to help customers enquire about vehicles available at our second-hand car showroom.

The service may automatically respond to WhatsApp messages containing vehicle names, models, or general vehicle enquiries.

2. Information We May Collect

When you communicate with us through WhatsApp, we may receive information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Your WhatsApp display name.
  • The messages you send to our WhatsApp number.
  • Vehicle-related enquiries and preferences contained in your messages.
  • Information necessary to respond to your enquiry.

We only use information that is relevant to providing our customer communication and vehicle enquiry service.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to vehicle availability enquiries.
  • Provide vehicle details such as model, price, year and mileage.
  • Send relevant vehicle photographs when appropriate.
  • Respond to customer requests and questions.
  • Provide customer support and follow-up communication.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

4. WhatsApp

Our service uses WhatsApp and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to communicate with customers.

Messages sent through WhatsApp are processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform and may be subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy .

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade customer personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the communication system, including WhatsApp/Meta and infrastructure providers used by the showroom application.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and rights of the showroom, customers or others.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect customer information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based communication system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Customer information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for providing the service, maintaining business records, resolving enquiries, complying with legal obligations, or maintaining system security.

8. Your Choices

Customers may stop receiving automated WhatsApp communications by sending STOP where supported by the service.

Customers may resume automated communication by sending START.

9. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your communications with the showroom.

To make a deletion request, contact us using the contact information below. We may need to verify the request before processing it.

10. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for customers who can legally use WhatsApp and purchase or enquire about vehicles. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your information, please contact the showroom through its official customer communication channels.
